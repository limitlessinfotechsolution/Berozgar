# Berozgar Structure \- Document

# **Berozgar — Official Website Screen & Page Architecture**

For **Berozgar**, I would structure the official website as more than a basic e-commerce storefront. The site should communicate the **brand identity first**, then convert visitors into customers through a streamlined shopping experience.

**Brand positioning:** Clothing / Streetwear / Fashion  
 **Tagline:** *“Unemployed For A Reason.”*  
 **Visual direction:** Bold · Rebellious · Premium · Minimal · Streetwear  
 **Core palette:** Black · White · Charcoal, with optional muted-rust/off-white accents.

## **Final Design Principle**

The most important distinction should be:

**Berozgar ≠ Generic E-commerce Website**

It should feel like:

**Brand Website \+ Streetwear Editorial \+ E-commerce Engine \+ Community**

The homepage should therefore be **campaign-driven**, not simply:

Banner → Categories → Products → Footer.

Instead:

CINEMATIC HERO  
       ↓  
"UNEMPLOYED FOR A REASON."  
       ↓  
CURRENT DROP  
       ↓  
EDITORIAL / CAMPAIGN  
       ↓  
SHOP THE COLLECTION  
       ↓  
BEST SELLERS  
       ↓  
BRAND MANIFESTO  
       ↓  
COMMUNITY / UGC  
       ↓  
JOURNAL  
       ↓  
NEWSLETTER  
       ↓  
FOOTER

This gives Berozgar a much stronger foundation for becoming a **distinctive Indian streetwear brand**, rather than another online clothing catalogue.
